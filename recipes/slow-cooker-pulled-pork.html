Slow Cooker Pulled Pork

Description

This slow cooker pulled pork recipe uses pork tenderloin and root beer to make the most tender shredded pork. Mixed with your favourite BBQ sauce, it's sure to bring rave reviews!

Ingredients

  • 1x 2-lbs pork tenderloin
  • 1x 12-fl. oz. can or bottle of root beer
  • 1x 18-oz. bottle of your favourite barbecue sauce
  • 8 hamburger buns, split and lightly toasted

Steps

  1. Gather the ingredients
  2. Place pork tenderloin into slow cooker; pour root beer over top.
  3. Cover and cook on low heat until pork shreds easily, about 6-7 hours. (Note that actual time may vary due to the individual slow cooker).
  4. Drain well. Stir in barbecue sauce.
  5. Pile pulled pork onto hamburger buns.
  6. Serve and enjoy!
