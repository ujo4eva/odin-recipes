Real N'awlins Muffuletta

Description

A genuine muffuletta should be made on oven-fresh round Italian bread loves topped with sesame seeds. The spicy, tangy olive salad is what really sets this meat and cheese sandwich apart. These gigantic sandwiches were invented a century ago at Sicilian Deli here in New Orleans. Be sure to use high-quality ingredients as it really makes a difference!

Ingredients

    Olive Salad
  • 1x cup pimento-stuffed green olives, crushed
  • 1/2x cup drained Kalamata olives, crushed
  • 1/2x cup pepperoncini, drained
  • 1/4x cup roughly chopped pickled cauliflower florets
  • 1/4x cup marinated cocktail onions
  • 2x cloves garlic, minced
  • 2x tbsp drained capers
  • 1x tbsp chopped celery
  • 1x tbsp chopped carrot
  • 1x tsp dried oregano
  • 1x tsp dried basil
  • 3/4x tsp ground black pepper
  • 1/2x tsp celery seeds
  • 1/2x cup olive oil
  • 1/4x cup canola oil
  • 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
    • Sandwiches
  • 2x 1lb loaves Italian bread
  • 8 oz. thinly sliced Genoa salami
  • 8 oz. thinly sliced cooked ham
  • 8 oz. sliced mortadella
  • 8 oz. sliced mozzarella cheese
  • 8 oz. sliced provolone cheese

Steps

  1. Roughly chop green olives, Kalamata olives, pepperoncini, cauliflower, cocktail onions, garlic, capers, celery, and carrot; transfer to a bowl.
  2. Stir oregano, basil, black pepper, and celery seeds into olive mixture. Stir in olive oil, canola oil, and vinegar; transfer olive salad to a glass jar (or other nonreactive container). Pour in more oil to cover if needed.
  3. Cover jar or container, refrigerate at least 8 hours to overnight.
  4. Cut bread loaves in half horizontally; hollow out some excess bread to make room for filling.
  5. Evenly divide olive salad, including oil, among bread pieces.
  6. Divide and layer salami, ham, mortadella, mozzarella, and provolone slices between bottom halves of each bread loaf.
  7. Place top halves of each loaf over layered meat and cheese. Cut sandwiches into quarters.
  8. Serve immediately, or wrap tightly and refrigerate for a few hours to allow the flavors to meld and the olive juice to soak into the bread.
